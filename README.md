# **Golden Baked Potato**

Version: 1.0

## **Overview**

Golden Baked Potato adds a new recovery-focused food item to Minecraft Bedrock Edition.  
Unlike Golden Apples, the Golden Baked Potato focuses on efficient healing rather than absorption hearts.

## **What it does**

Like a normal baked potato, it works like food, but it also regenerates 3 hearts with the regeneration 5 effect.

## **Why it exists**

I made this mode since I found out that Gapples waste absorption if you are not in PvP, so I made a mod that helps you heal in non-PVP situations like mining, etc., but don't use it in PvP, you would die earlier than a Gapple spammer.

## **Crafting Recipe**

Gold Nugget | Gold Ingot | Gold Nugget  
Gold Ingot | Baked Potato | Gold Ingot  
Gold Nugget | Gold Ingot | Gold Nugget

Required Materials:

* 4 Gold Ingots  
* 4 Gold Nuggets  
* 1 Baked Potato

## **Features**

* Custom item  
* Custom texture  
* Custom crafting recipe  
* Fast regeneration effect  
* Balanced alternative to Golden Apples

## **Installation**

Import both the Behavior Pack and Resource Pack into Minecraft Bedrock Edition and activate them in your world.
